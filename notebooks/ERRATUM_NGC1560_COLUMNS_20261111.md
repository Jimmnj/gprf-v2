## 1. The error
data/ngc1560/ngc1560_gentile2010_rc_extracted_20261108.csv labels column 5 "Vgas" and column 6 "Vdisk_ML1.43". They are the other way round: column 5 is the STELLAR curve (at M/L_I 1.43), column 6 is the GAS curve. Extraction error of 2026-11-08 (Claude).
## 2. Evidence (three independent lines)
(a) Ko et al. (2025) caption (main.tex l.144): V_gas dotted, V_disc dashed. In figs/NGC1560/Gentile/raw_data.pdf (viewed 17:1x) the dashed line is smooth, peaks near 25 km/s at 3.5 kpc and declines; the dotted line starts below zero, is wiggly and reaches 30 km/s at 7 kpc. Column 5 is the smooth curve; column 6 is the wiggly one.
(b) Column 6 is negative inside 0.5 kpc (-1.0, -2.2, -2.4). A central hole gives negative V^2 for HI, not for stars. The csv header explained it away as a "hollow stellar distribution".
(c) Enclosed masses at 9.53 kpc (V^2 r/G): column 6 gives 1.6e9 (x0.75 = 1.2e9, matching 1.33 M_HI = 1.10e9); column 5 gives 7.6e8 (matching Gentile's "around 5e8" at M/L 0.9, i.e. 7.9e8 at 1.43). Gentile's text: the wiggle is in V_gas.
