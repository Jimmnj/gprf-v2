## Question
At each arm's preferred intrinsic scatter, how do (D) a DC14 feedback-cored halo with the cosmological concentration prior and (K) a free Burkert halo compare in evidence with A (per-galaxy rho_c), U (universal rho_c), B (RAR function) and N (NFW, c-M prior)?

## Arms (cdm3_ns_20261031.py; same Gal.curves, nuisances, likelihood, nlive 300, dlogz 0.1, rwalk)
- D: DC14 (Di Cintio et al. 2014) (alpha,beta,gamma)(X), X = log10(M*/M200) clamped to [-4.1,-1.3] (validity range); M* = Ud L at the fitted Ud and distance. Priors: log M200 ~ U[8.5,14]; log c200(NFW) = Dutton-Maccio(M200) + delta, delta ~ N(0,0.11); c_DC14 = c_NFW (1 + 1e-5 exp[3.4 (X+4.5)]); c200 defined via r_-2 (as Li et al. 2020). Two halo parameters. Unit checks (18:40): at X=-4.1 reproduces NFW to 1-2%; M(<r200)=M200 exact; gamma 0.25 at X=-2.5.
- K: Burkert, log rho0 ~ U[5,10] Msun/kpc^3, log r_c ~ U[-1,2] kpc. Two free parameters, no cosmological prior: the "any core" ceiling.
- sig_int in {0.03, 0.06} (every arm so far prefers 0.03; 0 is omitted -- 0.06 is the robustness arm). 147 galaxies, 588 jobs. Output cdm3_ns_20261031.jsonl with logLmax, posterior medians, X and (alpha,beta,gamma) for D.

## Decisions
H1 A vs D: A favoured if G1 (A-D) > +5; D favoured if < -5; else indistinguishable. Same for A vs K.
H2 "feedback core closes the gap": yes if (D - N) > +5 AND G1 (A - D) < 0.5 x (A - N) [i.e. +455 shrinks by more than half].
H3 universal relation vs cored CDM: U favoured / D favoured / indistinguishable by the +-5 rule on (U - D).
H4 distances: D or K "bought with distances" if its > 2-sigma count exceeds 1.5 x N's (6).

## Expectations stated now
D beats N clearly (+100 to +300: cores on the LSBs). A vs D: indistinguishable to mildly A (|A-D| < 100), i.e. H2 YES. K (free Burkert) matches or beats A by a small margin: two free core parameters per galaxy are a ceiling the one-parameter relation should not clear. U loses to D by more than it loses to N (H3: D favoured). H4: NO for D (the c-M prior restrains it), possibly YES for K.
If A beats D by > +100 the relation's cored form is doing something DC14's feedback core does not; that would be the strong result and I do not expect it.

M* uses the disc term only (no bulge luminosity in the MRT), as the harness's M_b does for Sigma. Burkert has no cosmological prior by design; its evidence pays for two 5-dex and 3-dex flat priors. Nothing here bears on cosmological scales. Einasto not run (DC14 covers the feedback case; Burkert the empirical ceiling).
