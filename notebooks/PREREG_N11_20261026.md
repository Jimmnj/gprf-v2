## 2. Model (method (a), ruled 01:18)
- Each galaxy of baryonic mass M carries the isothermal tail with sigma^2 = sqrt(G a0 M)/2 (eq. 2). At Mpc radii
  the baryons and rho_c do not matter (tail independent of rho_c).
- Its EXCESS is rho_ex(r) = sigma^2/(2 pi G r^2) - rho_loc for r < r_t, zero beyond, with r_t = sigma/sqrt(2 pi G rho_loc)
  and excess mass M_ex = (4/3) sigma^2 r_t / G. rho_loc = the conditional mean nb density at r_t around that galaxy,
  EXCLUDING its own tail = background field rho_f + neighbours' excesses.
- Neighbours placed by the galaxy two-point function: conditional mean around a galaxy at distance r =
  rho_f + sum over mass classes M' of n(M') * integral d^3s [1 + xi_gg(s)] rho_ex,M'(|r - s|).
- Satellites (galaxies inside a more massive host's r_t) are truncated by the host's density (NEST-1 pass-through
  below LMC scale): handled by the same conditional-density rule, no separate tuning.
- Background field rho_f uniform (FLOOR-1 'smooths out'); fixed by the global constraint
      rho_f + sum_M n(M) M_ex(M) = rho_bar = 35.5.
- Iterate r_t(M), rho_loc(M), rho_f to convergence (mean-field; scatter about the conditional mean ignored in pass 1).
