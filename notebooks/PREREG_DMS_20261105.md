# PREREG_DMS_20261105 -- DiskMass Survey: vertical-dynamics baryon fractions against eq. (1) with no free parameter
Written 2026-11-05 19:40 EST BEFORE code. [UNRATIFIED]. Trigger: JM 18:04 "do both" (vertical test); data fetched by JM 19:12-19:18 (tools/fetch_diskmass_20261105.sh).
## 1. Question. DMS measures stellar vertical dispersions sigma_z and scale heights h_z in 30 face-on spirals, giving the dynamical surface density and hence a stellar M/L (Upsilon_K ~ 0.3, sub-maximal discs) INDEPENDENT of the rotation curve. From it DMS VII tabulates F_b = V_bary^2/V_tot^2 at R = 2.2 h_R (Chap5_tabF; Case I, nominal Upsilon) and the baryonic masses (Chap5_tabMasses). Angus et al. 2015 showed MOND cannot match: it needs Upsilon_K ~ 0.55 (F_b = 1 by construction) and then over-predicts sigma_z unless h_z is halved. Does eq. (1), which supplies real nb mass in the inner disc, predict the measured F_b with NO free parameter?
## 2. Inputs (fixed). arxiv_1308.0336_src/tables: Chap5_tabMasses (log M_disk*, M_bulge*, M_atom, M_mol, M_bary; Case I), tab_hRhz (h_R, h_z kpc), Chap5_tabF (F_b at 2.2 h_R with error; F_b,max), Chap5_tabDM (Upsilon_K, pISO R_C, log rho_0; Case I), Chap5_tabModHI (D_HI where given). arxiv_1307.8130_src/tables: Chap2_tab5 (V_arot^[OIII] and i_TF; <V_c sin i>_w gas), tab6 (sigma_z,0, h_sigz). Universal rho_c = 10^7.45 Msun/kpc^3; a0 = 1.2e-10; ambient 35.5.
## 3. Model (fixed). Baryons: stellar disc = razor-thin exponential (M_disk*, h_R; Freeman curve); bulge = point mass M_bulge* (small, central); atomic gas = exponential disc with scale length 2 h_R and mass 1.33 M_atom (helium; DMS M_atom is HI -- check their definition and do NOT double-count He if included); molecular gas = exponential with h_R, mass M_mol. v_b^2(r) summed; baryon potential by the pass2 integral; eq. (1) solved spherically as in pass2 solve_gal (sigma from the total M_b through eq. 2, ambient subtracted). Output per galaxy: F_b,pred = v_b^2/v_tot^2 at 2.2 h_R; V_flat,pred = (G a0 M_b)^{1/4}; model V at the last DMS radius.
## 4. Criteria (fixed now)
T1 F_b: median (F_b,pred - F_b,DMS) within +-0.10 AND rms of the difference <= 0.15 (DMS errors ~0.06-0.2) -> PASS (parameter-free match). Median offset > +0.10 (model gives too little nb mass in the inner disc at universal rho_c) -> report as "DMS discs need rho_c above the SPARC universal value by X dex" (the X computed post hoc by rescaling rho_c to match, labelled so). Median < -0.10 -> too much nb mass; report.
T2 BTFR with the sigma_z-based baryonic masses: median log(V_arot^[OIII] / V_flat,pred) within +-0.03 and rms <= 0.08 -> PASS. (MOND makes the same V prediction from M_b but requires a larger M_b to fit the inner curve; the relation does not.)
T3 (report only): 4 pi G rho_0 R_C^2 (pISO asymptotic v^2, Case I) vs 2 sigma^2 of the relation.
Expectation (Claude): T1 median offset +0.05 to +0.15 (universal rho_c is low for massive spirals: SPARC fitted rho_c for log M_b ~ 10.5 sits near 7.5-7.7), rms ~0.12; T2 PASS. Either way MOND's F_b = 1 against DMS 0.4-0.8 is the contrast the result is read against.
