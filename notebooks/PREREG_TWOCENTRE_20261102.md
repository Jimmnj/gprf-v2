## 1. Question
Under ONE-FIELD, the LMC has no nb response of its own. Its baryons enter the Milky Way's phi_b and modulate the Milky Way's response (sigma_MW, rho_c,MW). How much nb mass does the relation place around the LMC, above the unperturbed Milky Way profile, and how does that compare with (i) the linearised 1.36 nesting factor now in the note, (ii) the two-halo reading (LMC own sigma, tail cut at the MW density; ~4e10, note sec 7), (iii) the measured perturber mass 1-2e11 (Erkal+2019, Vasiliev 2021, Chandra+2025 -- numbers to be verified under item 4 of the handoff, not trusted from memory)?

## 2. Model (fixed before running)
- Law: rho_nb = rho_c exp[S(x_b) - S(x_b0) - dphi_nb/sigma^2] - rho_amb, S(x) = 2 tanh(x/2), x_b = phi_b/sigma^2 (drop from the MW centre), floor at -rho_amb. Poisson with rho_b + rho_nb. One sigma: sigma_MW. One rho_c: rho_c,MW. x_b0 = MW central value (LMC adds ~nothing there).
- sigma_MW^2 = 1/2 sqrt(G a0 M_b,MW), M_b,MW = 6.15e10 (Ou+2024 baryon model, RESULT_MW_DECLINE) -> sigma 125.1 km/s.
- rho_c,MW: PRIMARY universal 10^7.45; SECONDARY fitted 10^7.88 (RESULT_MW_DECLINE). rho_amb = 35.5 Msun/kpc^3.
- MW baryons: spherical equivalent (Hernquist bulge 1.55e10 a=0.70; discs 3.65e10 + 8.2e9 + 1.3e9 as spherical exponential-equivalents with the same half-mass radii). Justification: the quantity sought lives at 40-60 kpc where the MW baryon field is the monopole to <1%; Gate 2b showed the nb response is monopole-dominated and q>=0.9 beyond 5 Rd. The MW disc axis is therefore dropped and the solve is axisymmetric about the MW-LMC line. Recorded as an approximation; checked by one run with the MW discs as thin discs along the symmetry axis (worst-case orientation) IF the primary result sits within a factor 2 of a decision boundary.
- LMC baryons: Plummer, M_b 3.0e9, a 1.5 kpc, centre at D = 50 kpc on the symmetry axis (same inputs as RESULT_RULEA sec LMC). Secondary: stars 2.7e9 (a 1.5) + gas 0.5e9 (a 3.0) exponential-equivalent, M_b 3.2e9.
- Geometry: spherical coordinates about the MW centre, nb potential by multipole Green's functions l = 0..L (L = 40 primary; L = 24 and 60 for convergence), radial log grid 0.05-3000 kpc, Gauss-Legendre in mu. phi_b analytic (MW spherical + LMC Plummer, exact). Picard iteration with under-relaxation from the unperturbed spherical MW solve (shooting, as mw_decline); converged when max |delta rho_nb|/rho_nb < 1e-4 and the enclosed-mass diagnostics move < 0.1%. If Picard fails to converge (Jeans length at the LMC's position sigma/sqrt(4 pi G rho_nb) ~ 30 kpc makes the +k^2 oscillatory response possible), switch to Newton-Krylov on the same grid; record the switch.
- Validity controls (must pass before results are read): V1 LMC mass set to 0 reproduces the mw_decline spherical curve (v_c at 8-27 kpc to 0.1 km/s; M_nb(<50) to 0.1%). V2 LMC moved to 500 kpc: excess within 50 kpc of the LMC -> the isolated-perturbation value, and the MW profile unchanged. V3 L convergence: excess(<30 kpc) changes < 2% from L=40 to L=60.

## 3. Outputs
For each run: M_exc(<R) = integral over the sphere of radius R about the LMC centre of [rho_nb(two-centre) - rho_nb(MW alone)], R = 10, 30, 50 kpc; M_LMC,model(<R) = M_b,LMC(<R) + M_exc(<R); the peak density ratio at the LMC centre rho_nb(two)/rho_nb(MW alone) (= the full-form nesting factor); the MW circular speed at 8-27 kpc with and without the LMC (reflex-free check that the MW fit is not disturbed). Comparison columns computed by the SAME code and radii: (i) linearised factor: M_MWnb(<R about LMC) x [exp(S(phi_LMC/sigma_MW^2)) - 1] with phi_LMC the Plummer potential; (ii) two-halo reading: LMC own-sigma (58.8) isothermal solve at rho_c 10^7.45, tail cut where its density meets the MW's at 50 kpc, plus the 1.36 factor (RULEA inputs). Table to RESULT_TWOCENTRE_20261102.md and GPRF_TwoCentre_20261102.xlsx.
