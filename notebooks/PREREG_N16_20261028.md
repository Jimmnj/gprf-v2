## 1. Hypothesis (the closure)

Set the King core surface density equal to Milgrom's (2009) core surface density, galaxy by galaxy:

    rho_c r_c = mu0_M(Sigma_b),   r_c = 3 sigma / sqrt(4 pi G rho_c)
    =>  rho_c = 4 pi G mu0_M^2 / (9 sigma^2),   sigma^2 = (1/2) sqrt(G a0 M_b)  [eq. (2)]

mu0_M from Milgrom 2009 eq. 11, exactly as in fig4_mu0_mond_20261027.py:
- Sigma_c* = Sigma_M {(6/pi) X0 [1 - mu(X0)] + int_0^X0 L dx},  X0 mu(X0) = (2/3) Sigma_b/Sigma_M,  L = x mu'/mu.
- Sigma_M = a0/2piG. mu = RAR mu (inverse of nu(y) = 1/(1 - e^-sqrt y)); lambda = 3.29, gamma = 2.09.
- Sigma_b = 0.75 Sigma_h, Sigma_h = M_b/(2 pi R_h^2), R_h = 1.678 R_d (dwarfs: R_h = r_h, as in B.2).
- No free constant. No f_gas term. No refit of anything.

Identification caveat (stated now, not after): this sets the King product rho_c r_c equal to a quantity that Donato/Milgrom define for a Burkert-type core (rho_0 r_0). Fig 4 already compares the two on one axis. A factor of order unity between them is NOT allowed as a free constant in the verdict run. (Section 6 reports what constant would be needed, as a diagnostic only.)
