(c) FEATURE (Renzo's rule, the real risk): detrend V_obs, V_bar, V_R1, V_R3 by a per-curve quadratic in log r (v2.0.1 sec 7 recipe) over 2 <= r <= 8 kpc; feature amplitude A = peak-to-trough of the detrended log V in 3 <= r <= 6.5 kpc (the gas peak at 3.4 kpc and its print-through). PASS-F: A(R1) within a factor 2 of A(V_obs) AND of the same sign (peak at the gas peak). NULL-F: A(V_obs) < 2x its noise (median sigma_V/V_obs in the window) -> Gentile's curve has no scorable feature (2508.03569's finding); report, no score. FAIL-F: A(V_obs) significant and A(R1) < A(V_obs)/2 -> the Poisson smoothing erases the print-through that MOND (R3) keeps; goes in "where it fails". Also report A(R3) and the Pearson r of detrended V_R1 vs V_obs against V_R3 vs V_obs.
(d) OUTER SLOPE: d log V / d log r over the last 5 points: observed vs R1 vs R3. Report only (the curve has no flat part; sec 3.1 (iii) territory).
