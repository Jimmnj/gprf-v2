## Changes from v2.0.1 (10.5281/zenodo.23815559)

Method
1. Fitted densities. Each galaxy's rho_c is the first minimum of chi^2, counting up from the lowest density tried (Section 2.2). A fit whose chi^2 at the lowest density tried lies below every interior minimum on the first, lower-density branch counts as an upper limit; no galaxy in the table meets that condition. All statistics of the fitted densities were recomputed.
2. Second solutions. In four galaxies (NGC 0289, NGC 5907, NGC 2998, UGC 11914) chi^2 has a second minimum 3.3-3.6 dex higher in density. Section 2.2 and Appendix I identify the repetition as the Joseph-Lundgren oscillation (JLO): period 8 pi/sqrt(7) in ln rho_c, 4.1 dex; between the second and third minima we measure 4.05-4.15.
3. Sample. Statistics use the 141 galaxies with at least three fitted points. Six are cut: CamB, D512-2, NGC 4068, NGC 6789, UGC 05750, UGC 07232. Table 2 still lists all 147.
4. Spread. The scatter of the fitted densities is a robust spread, half the 16-84 per cent range: 0.5 dex about a median of 10^7.42 (141 galaxies). v2.0.1 quoted a standard deviation, 0.68 dex.
5. Dwarf protocol. The Plummer scale radius is the projected half-light radius. The catalogue radii are projected; v2.0.1's code treated them as 3-D radii and divided them by 1.305.
6. Definition. rho_c is the central nb density of eq. (1) for the smooth baryon model (stars and gas), one number per galaxy. This is now stated in Section 2.1.
7. Arms (Appendix B.1). Two arms are reported, and every rho_c number names its arm. The fixed arm uses the catalogue mass-to-light ratio, distance and inclination; the marginalised arm lets them vary within their errors. Per galaxy they differ by +-0.4 dex (levers rho_c proportional to D^-5 and to (V_f/V_BTFR)^6). v2.0.1 did not name the arm (items 11-14).
